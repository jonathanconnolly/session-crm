Ask us anything →
What if my child won’t cooperate?

We don’t bribe kids to smile or act silly — and we definitely don’t say “cheese.” Our approach is calm, honest, and all about letting your child just be. The best thing you can do? Sit back, relax, and trust the process. This is about personality, not perfection!

How many kids are allowed?

Each 15 minute session is ideal for 1-2 children (ages 6mo–18yrs), but you know them best and what they will need!

Can I get sibling photos?

Our main focus is capturing each child individually — their personality, expression, and full range of emotion. That’s what makes this different from anything you’ve done before. If time allows, we’ll try to get a sibling photo at the end, but we can’t guarantee it in one session. Some sessions flow smoothly and we get everything; others can be more unpredictable, especially with younger kids. If sibling photos are really important to you, we highly recommend booking a second session just for that, so you get the time and focus needed without feeling rushed. We come back often, so you don’t have to do it all in one session.

What about family photos?

Unfortunately, we do not offer any family portraits at this time.

What to wear?

Most shots will be chest up so you don't have to worry about the entire wardrobe. We highly recommend neutral tones or soft colors without graphics. White or Black are always great options. Keep it simple and timeless.

Do you include color and B&W?

Yes, we include both color and black & white options in your final gallery. While many photographers only deliver overexposed black & white portraits, we believe in preserving the real details — like your child’s skin tone, eye color, freckles, and those perfect imperfections that make them who they are. Our editing style is natural, timeless, and intentional, developed over 18+ years to stand the test of time. No trendy filters or harsh edits — just beautiful, honest portraits you’ll love forever.

Where is the studio?

We bring a complete pop-up studio to your city for one weekend. For the safety and privacy of the families we photograph, we never publish the address. The exact location is emailed to you as soon as your booking is complete. If it turns out to be too far, you’re welcome to cancel within 24 hours for a full refund, no matter what.

Cancellation policy?

A non-refundable retainer (booking fee) is required to reserve your time slot. If you change your mind within 24 hours of booking, your retainer can be refunded. If you need to reschedule, please let us know at least 7 days in advance. In the event of illness, family emergencies, or other extenuating circumstances please contact us at your earliest convenience.

What if all spots are booked?

If a pop-up is fully booked, please join the waitlist on the specific city booking page. If there are any cancellations, you'll be notified first.
