What is the price?

Since we are a travel based studio not all events are priced the same. Most families spend between $500 and $750 on their session, with prints available on top of that. For details on what's included, see the offer on your city's booking page.

How many kids are allowed?

Each 15 minute session is ideal for 1-2 children (ages 6mo–18yrs), but you know them best and what they will need!

What is your approach like?

We don’t bribe kids to smile or act silly — and we definitely don’t say “cheese.” Our approach is calm, honest, and all about letting your child just be. No forced poses. No fake expressions. Just real personality, captured naturally. The best thing you can do? Sit back, relax, and trust the process. This is about personality, not perfection!

When will you be in my city?

To be the first to know when we'll be in your city, fill out the Find Out First form below or join the waitlist on any city booking page.
