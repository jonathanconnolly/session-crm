Questions

Good to know

What does it cost, and what's included?

The Jacksonville Personality Pop-Up™ Session is $595. It includes a 15-minute session with a certified Personality Method™ photographer, 25+ high-resolution images (every one in color and black & white) and a private online gallery to download, print and share. A $150 deposit reserves your time; the rest is charged 14 days before your session.

How does payment work?

A non-refundable $150 deposit reserves your time. The remaining $445 is charged automatically to the same card 14 days before your session, and we email you before it runs.

What if I change my mind or need to reschedule?

If you change your mind within 24 hours of booking, your deposit can be refunded. If you need to reschedule, please let us know at least 7 days in advance. In the event of illness, family emergencies, or other extenuating circumstances please contact us at your earliest convenience.

What if the day I want is sold out?

Tap “Join the waitlist” under that day in the booking box. If a spot opens up, you’ll be texted first. Can’t make January 9–10 at all? Leave your email above and you’ll be the first to hear when our next Jacksonville dates open.

Where is the studio?

We bring a complete portrait studio to Jacksonville for one weekend. The exact address is in your booking confirmation.

How many kids are allowed?

Each 15 minute session is ideal for 1-2 children (ages 6mo–18yrs), but you know them best and what they will need!

Can I get sibling photos?

Our main focus is capturing each child individually — their personality, expression, and full range of emotion. That’s what makes this different from anything you’ve done before. If time allows, we’ll try to get a sibling photo at the end, but we can’t guarantee it in one session. If sibling photos are really important to you, we highly recommend adding a second session, so you get the time and focus needed without feeling rushed.

What about family photos?

Unfortunately, we do not offer any family portraits at this time. Sessions are all about the kids.

What should they wear?

Most shots will be chest up so you don't have to worry about the entire wardrobe. We highly recommend neutral tones or soft colors without graphics. White or Black are always great options. Keep it simple and timeless.

What should I bring, and when should we arrive?

Wipes, chapstick, and one favorite toy/lovey. Arrive 10 minutes early so your child can get comfortable.

Do you include color and black & white?

Yes, we include both color and black & white options in your final gallery. While many photographers only deliver overexposed black & white portraits, we believe in preserving the real details — like your child’s skin tone, eye color, freckles, and those perfect imperfections that make them who they are. Our editing style is natural, timeless, and intentional, developed over 18+ years to stand the test of time. No trendy filters or harsh edits — just beautiful, honest portraits you’ll love forever.

Who will be photographing my child?

We’re a boutique team of all-female traveling kid photographers, certified in the Personality Method™ — and we’re not just photographers. We’re moms, teachers, and women who genuinely love kids. We don’t make it about our art — we make it about your child. What matters most is capturing real moments, honoring who they are right now, and creating an experience you’ll both remember. We’re patient, empathetic, and fully present!

What is your approach like?

We don’t bribe kids to smile or act silly — and we definitely don’t say “cheese.” Our approach is calm, honest, and all about letting your child just be. No forced poses. No fake expressions. Just real personality, captured naturally. The best thing you can do? Sit back, relax, and trust the process. This is about personality, not perfection!
