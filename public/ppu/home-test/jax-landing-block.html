Ask us anything →
What does it cost, and what’s included?

The Jacksonville Personality Pop-Up™ Session is $595: a 15-minute session with a certified Personality Method™ photographer, 25+ high-resolution images (every one in color and black & white) and a private online gallery to download, print and share. A $150 deposit reserves your time; the $445 balance is charged automatically 14 days before your session, and we email you before it runs.

What if I change my mind or need to reschedule?

If you change your mind within 24 hours of booking, your deposit can be refunded. If you need to reschedule, please let us know at least 7 days in advance. In the event of illness, family emergencies, or other extenuating circumstances please contact us at your earliest convenience.

What if my child won’t cooperate?

We don’t bribe kids to smile or act silly — and we definitely don’t say “cheese.” Our approach is calm, honest, and all about letting your child just be. The best thing you can do? Sit back, relax, and trust the process. This is about personality, not perfection!

How many kids are allowed?

Each 15 minute session is ideal for 1-2 children (ages 6mo–18yrs), but you know them best and what they will need! Our focus is capturing each child individually. If time allows, we’ll try for a sibling photo at the end — and if sibling photos really matter to you, add a second session so there’s time to do it right.

What should they wear, and what should I bring?

Most shots will be chest up so you don't have to worry about the entire wardrobe. We highly recommend neutral tones or soft colors without graphics. White or Black are always great options. Keep it simple and timeless. Bring wipes, chapstick, and one favorite toy or lovey, and arrive 10 minutes early so your child can get comfortable.

Where is the studio?

We bring a complete portrait studio to Jacksonville for one weekend. The exact address is in your booking confirmation.

What if the day I want is sold out?

Tap “Join the waitlist” under that day in the booking box — if a spot opens up, you’ll be texted first. Can’t make January 9–10 at all? Leave your email above and you’ll be the first to hear when our next Jacksonville dates open.

Do you include color and black & white?

Yes, we include both color and black & white options in your final gallery. While many photographers only deliver overexposed black & white portraits, we believe in preserving the real details — like your child’s skin tone, eye color, freckles, and those perfect imperfections that make them who they are. Our editing style is natural, timeless, and intentional, developed over 18+ years to stand the test of time. No trendy filters or harsh edits — just beautiful, honest portraits you’ll love forever.
