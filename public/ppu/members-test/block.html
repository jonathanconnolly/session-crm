Questions

Before you join.

Ask us anything →
What’s the difference between the two?

Access opens the calendar: every date the moment it exists, and you book before anyone else. You pay for sessions separately, at whatever your city charges that year. Access Plus adds the session itself, bought now at one fixed price of $595 no matter which city you see us in, with the date decided later.

Why does Plus lock the price?

Because a session isn’t the same price everywhere. Depending on the city it runs a few hundred dollars either side of $595, and those prices move over time. Plus fixes yours at $595 for as long as you stay a member, wherever you happen to catch us.

Can I pay monthly?

Both plans are billed once a year. We show Access as a monthly figure because that’s the easiest way to think about it, but it comes out in one payment. Paying by the month would mean someone could use a session in February and stop paying in March, and that isn’t fair to the families who stay.

When do I have to use my sessions?

Within your membership year. You don’t have to pick the date when you join, and you can move it as often as you need, but the sessions you buy for a year are meant for that year. That’s also why we cap it at two: we’d rather you use what you buy than stockpile.

We’ve booked with you before. What changes?

The session is exactly the one you already love. What changes is everything around it: the full calendar instead of one weekend, the first pick of times, and reminders so it never sneaks up on you. On Plus, there’s also nothing left to pay when you book.

How does booking work?

You sign in and the calendar is there. Pick your weekend and your 15-minute time, months ahead if you like. Members pick before anything goes public. Change or move your time whenever you need to.

Can I cancel?

Any time before it renews, from the link in your receipt or by emailing us. Your access runs to the end of the year you paid for, and a Plus session you’ve already paid for stays yours until that year is up. You keep every image you’ve ever received.

Which cities does it work in?

All of them. Your membership is good at any Personality Pop-Up, wherever we are, and that’s rather the point of the fixed price on Plus. At checkout we ask which city you usually see us in, so we can tell you first when those dates open. See where we go →
