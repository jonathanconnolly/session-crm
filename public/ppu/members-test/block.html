Before you join

Questions parents ask.

Ask us anything →
What am I paying for today?

$295 a year, which does two things: it opens the year’s dates to you before they are public, and it holds a spot for you in the season you choose. It also comes straight off the price of that session.

When do I pay the rest?

14 days before you shoot. The remaining $300 is charged then, so a session you booked eight months ago is not paid for until it is nearly here.

What does a session cost in total?

$595, wherever we see you. Sessions normally run $495 to $749 depending on the city, and those prices rise. Yours is locked while you stay a member.

Can I have more than one session a year?

Yes. Choose one, two, three or four a year. The membership is $295 for each, so two a year is $590, three is $885 and four is $1,180. Each one is held in your season.

Do I pick the exact date now?

No, and that is the point. You choose the season. When we set those dates in your city, you pick your time before anyone else sees them.

What if the dates still do not work?

Move to another season or another city, as often as you need. Nothing is lost and nothing expires while you are a member.

What if you do not come to my city?

Your membership is good at any Personality Pop-Up, anywhere we go. If we never reach you, tell us and we refund the year in full.

Can I cancel?

Any time before it renews. You keep the session you have already paid for that year, and every image you have ever received.

How many kids are in a session?

Each 15 minute session suits 1 to 2 children, ages 6 months to 18 years. You know them best.
