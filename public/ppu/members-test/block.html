Ask us anything →
What exactly do I get?

One Personality Pop-Up™ Session every membership year — the same $595 session everyone else books: 15 minutes with a certified Personality Method™ photographer, 25+ high-resolution images (every one in color and black & white) and a private online gallery to download, print and share. Plus the two things money usually can’t buy in Dallas: a guaranteed spot and first access to the times.

How do I book as a member?

The moment our next Dallas dates are set, members get a text and an email with a private booking link — 48 hours before booking opens to everyone else. Pick your time like any booking, except there’s no deposit and no balance: your membership covers the session. Same photographers, same studio, same 1–2 kids per session.

Yearly or monthly — what’s the difference?

It’s the same membership. Yearly is $495 once a year — $100 less than booking a session at the door. Monthly spreads it out at $49 a month for 12 months ($588 a year), so a session never hits your card all at once. Both include one session a year, the guaranteed spot and first access.

What if I can’t make the Dallas dates?

No stress. Your session credit stays on your account and waits for our next Dallas dates — it doesn’t expire while your membership is active. Use it this visit or the next; it’s yours.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time before your renewal and nothing more is charged — you keep every image you’ve received. Monthly memberships run for their first 12 months (that’s the session you’ve already been able to use), then you can cancel anytime. Manage everything from the link in your receipt, or just email us.

Will the price go up?

Not for you. Founding Dallas members keep today’s price — $495 a year or $49 a month — for as long as the membership stays active, even as session prices change.

Is membership only for Dallas?

For now, yes — Dallas is where we’re starting, with a small founding group of 20 families. Your session credit is good at any Dallas Personality Pop-Up. If you’d love this in your city, tell us →
