Questions

Before you do.

Ask us anything →
I’ve already shot with you. What’s different?

The session is exactly the one you know — same studio, same team, same fifteen minutes. What changes is the order of things. Instead of waiting to see whether we announce a weekend that works, you tell us the season, and we come to you with those dates before anyone else sees them.

What am I actually paying for?

Your session, started early. The $300 comes straight off the price — it isn’t a fee, a membership, or a booking charge. When you book, you pay the remaining $295 and that’s the whole cost.

Do I have to know the date now?

No — that’s the point. A season is as specific as you need to be. We’ll come to you when those dates are set and you pick the day and time then, with the whole weekend still open.

What if the dates still don’t work?

Move it. Another season, another city, as many times as you need. Your deposit stays on your account with no expiry date. This is the part most places get wrong and it’s the reason we built it this way.

What if you don’t come to my city?

Your deposit and your fixed price are good at any Personality Pop-Up, anywhere we go. Plenty of families catch us in a neighbouring city or while they’re travelling. Tell us your city when you check out and we’ll tell you first about everything within reach of you. See where we go →

What if the price goes up?

Not for you. Sessions run a few hundred dollars either side of $595 depending on the city, and those numbers climb. Yours is fixed at $595 from the day you put the deposit down, however far out you end up booking.

Can I get it back?

Email us within 14 days and we’ll refund it, no questions. After that it stays as credit toward a session rather than coming back to your card — but it never expires and you can move it as often as you like.

Can I hold more than one?

Yes. Some families hold two in a year, or one each for siblings shooting separately. Put down a deposit for each and tell us the seasons — one session covers one or two kids together, so you only need a second if you want a second sitting.
