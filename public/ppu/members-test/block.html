Ask us anything →
What do I get?

The number of sessions you choose, every year, at today’s member price, locked in for as long as you stay. Each session is the same one everyone else books: 15 minutes with a certified Personality Method™ photographer, 25+ high-resolution images in color and black & white, and a private gallery to download, print and share. Plus a guaranteed spot and first access to times at every pop-up in your city.

What if I don’t use a session this year?

It rolls over. Unused sessions stay on your account for as long as you’re a member, so you can use two next year, or save one for a sibling. Nothing expires while your membership is active.

How many sessions should I choose?

One session covers 1 or 2 kids, so most families choose one per visit. Choose Family for two sessions: siblings on their own, or two visits a year. Need three or four? Change the quantity at checkout. You can add sessions any time.

How do I book as a member?

When dates are set for your city, you get a text and an email with a private booking link, 48 hours before booking opens to everyone else. Pick your time like any booking, except there’s no deposit and no balance. Your membership covers it.

Yearly or monthly?

Same membership, same sessions. Yearly is $570 per session, paid once a year (that’s $47.50 a month). Monthly is $50 per session each month, so nothing big ever hits your card. Both renew automatically, and you can switch or cancel any time.

Will my price go up?

No. Session prices go up over time, but your member price is locked for as long as your membership stays active. That’s the main reason families join now.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from the link in your receipt or by emailing us. Nothing more is charged, and any session you’ve already paid for stays yours to use for 12 months. You keep every image you’ve received.

Which cities?

Any city in the list above. Your sessions are good at every Personality Pop-Up in the city you choose, and if you move, just tell us. Don’t see your city? Find out first →
