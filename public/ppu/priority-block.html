Before you reserve

Questions parents ask.

Ask us anything →
What is Personality Pop-Up?

A traveling portrait studio for kids. We set up in one city at a time for a weekend, and every session is 15 minutes long. No posing, no cheese, no perfect kids required.

What is the Priority Pass?

It is your place at the front of the booking line for a season you choose. When we announce dates for your city, pass holders pick their time before anyone else. It is $50 a session, fully refundable, and it comes off your session total.

Is this a confirmed appointment?

No. The pass holds your place in line. You pick your time once dates are announced for your city.

Is the $50 refundable?

Yes. Ask any time, for any reason, and we refund it in full.

Does my $50 go toward my session?

Yes. The full $50 comes off your session total.

What does a session cost?

Most families spend $500 to $750 on their session, plus prints on top of that.

How many kids are allowed?

Each 15 minute session is ideal for 1 to 2 children, ages 6 months to 18 years. You know them best.

What if my child will not cooperate?

We never bribe kids to smile and we never say cheese. Sit back and trust the process. This is about personality, not perfection.

What if you do not visit my city?

Take a full refund, or hold the credit for any city we visit.

Am I guaranteed a session?

No. You book before the public, subject to availability. A weekend holds 40 sessions and they fill.
